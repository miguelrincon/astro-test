## A gentler way to change

You do not need to be fixed. You need a little space, a little guidance, and a way to work with the part of your mind that has been running the same loop for years.

Hypnotherapy uses focused attention and deep relaxation to reach the patterns that sit just below everyday thinking. In that relaxed state, old associations become easier to soften and new responses become easier to rehearse. It is not magic, and it is not sleep. It is a practical, collaborative way to help your nervous system learn a different way forward.

I work with people who are tired of managing symptoms and want to understand what is keeping them stuck. Sessions are calm, conversational and always at your pace. You stay aware and in control throughout.

Whether you want to feel less anxious, sleep through the night, speak up with confidence or finally change a habit, we start with what matters most to you — and build from there.
