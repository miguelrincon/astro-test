## How pricing works

The first 20-minute intake is always free and without obligation. A standard session is 60 minutes and costs €95. Payment is due on the day of the session by card or bank transfer.

Most people book a block of three or six sessions. This is not required, but it gives the work a rhythm and is slightly cheaper. Sessions are valid for six months.

## Packages

- **Single session** — €95. Useful for a specific event or a first taste of the work.
- **Three sessions** — €270 (€90 per session). A good starting point for most goals.
- **Six sessions** — €510 (€85 per session). For deeper patterns or long-standing habits.

## Practical details

- Sessions take place in Rotterdam or online via video.
- Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance to avoid being charged.
- If you are running late, the session still ends at the agreed time.
- I do not work with health insurers directly; you will receive an invoice that you may be able to submit depending on your policy.
- If cost is a barrier, ask. I keep a small number of reduced-fee places each month.
