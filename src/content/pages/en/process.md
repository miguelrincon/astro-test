## Before we meet

You book a free 20-minute intake through the calendar. There is nothing to prepare. If you have questions, write them down or bring them — they are welcome.

## The free intake

We meet online or at the practice. I ask what is going on, what you have already tried, and what you would like to be different. You can ask me anything about how I work. By the end, we both have a sense of whether this is a good fit, and what a realistic plan looks like.

There is no obligation to book a session afterwards. Sometimes the most useful outcome is a recommendation to see someone else.

## A typical session

Each session lasts 60 minutes and follows a gentle rhythm:

1. **Check-in.** We talk about how the past week has been and what you want from today.
2. **Focus.** We choose one clear direction — not everything at once.
3. **Relaxation.** I guide you into a comfortable, focused state with a slow spoken induction.
4. **The work.** We explore the thoughts, images or memories connected to the change, and rehearse new responses.
5. **Return.** I bring you back gently, and we talk briefly about what you noticed.
6. **Practice.** You leave with one small, specific thing to try before we meet again.

## After the session

You may feel deeply relaxed, a little floaty, or simply quiet. Give yourself a few minutes before driving or making decisions. Drink some water. Most people sleep well that night.

Change often arrives quietly: a moment where you would normally react, and you notice a little more choice. We track those moments together and build on them.

## Between sessions

A short daily practice — often five minutes of the relaxation we used, or a simple mental rehearsal — makes the biggest difference. I will record a version for you to keep.
