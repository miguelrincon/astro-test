## Services

Stillwater Hypnotherapy provides hypnotherapy and related coaching for personal development. Hypnotherapy is not a substitute for medical, psychiatric or psychological diagnosis or treatment. If you are unsure whether this service is appropriate for you, please consult your GP or specialist first.

## Bookings and cancellations

Appointments can be booked online or by email. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Later cancellations or no-shows may be charged at the full session rate. If you are late, the session will still end at the scheduled time.

## Payment

The free intake is free of charge. Sessions are €95 for 60 minutes unless agreed otherwise in writing. Payment is due on the day of the session by card or bank transfer. Packages are valid for six months from the date of purchase.

## Confidentiality

Everything discussed in sessions is confidential, within the limits of the law and professional ethics. If there is a serious risk to your safety or the safety of others, or a legal obligation to disclose, we will discuss this with you wherever possible.

## Liability

## Complaints

If something does not feel right, please tell us as soon as possible so we can address it. We are also a member of a professional association with an independent complaints procedure, details of which are available on request.
