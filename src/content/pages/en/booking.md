## What happens after you book

You will receive a confirmation email with a video link or the practice address. If you need to move the appointment, you can do so up to 24 hours beforehand.

The intake is a conversation, not a session. You do not need to prepare, and you do not have to decide anything on the call. If we decide to work together, we will schedule your first full session at the end.
