## Why I do this work

Most people arrive at hypnotherapy after trying very hard to think their way out of something. They have read the books, made the plans, perhaps seen other practitioners. What they often need is not more effort, but a different way of relating to their own mind.

I am fascinated by the quiet, automatic patterns that shape how we feel and respond — and by how gently those patterns can shift when we work with them directly. My role is not to tell you who to be. It is to help you hear yourself more clearly, and to turn that clarity into something you can practise.

## Training and background

I trained as a clinical hypnotherapist in the Netherlands and hold a diploma in therapeutic hypnosis. My work is grounded in contemporary understanding of stress, memory and habit formation, and I continue to study with supervisors and peers each year.

I am a member of a professional hypnotherapy association and work within its code of ethics and complaints procedure. I also hold professional indemnity insurance and follow regular supervision.

## How I work

- **Collaborative.** You are the expert on your life. I bring tools, structure and a calm space to think.
- **Practical.** Every session ends with something you can use between appointments.
- **Paced.** We go only as fast as feels useful. You can pause, ask questions or change direction at any point.
- **Respectful.** I do not diagnose medical conditions or replace medical or psychological care. When something needs another specialist, I will say so.

## Outside the practice

When I am not working, you will usually find me walking somewhere green, reading about the mind, or making slow coffee. I bring the same unhurried attention to my sessions.
