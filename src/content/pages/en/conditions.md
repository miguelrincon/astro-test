## Common reasons people come

### Anxiety, worry and panic

Racing thoughts, a tight chest, a mind that scans for danger even when nothing is wrong. We work with the alarm response itself, helping your body learn that it can stand down.

### Stress and burnout

Feeling wired but exhausted, unable to switch off, or disconnected from the things you used to enjoy. We look at the pace you are running at and rehearse a more sustainable way of responding.

### Sleep and insomnia

A busy mind at bedtime, waking at three in the morning, or feeling tired no matter how long you lie there. We combine deep relaxation with practical changes to your evening routine.

### Confidence and self-esteem

Holding back in meetings, shrinking in social situations, or carrying an old story about not being enough. We gently update that story and rehearse the version of you that feels more true.

### Phobias and fears

Flying, public speaking, needles, spiders, lifts. We work with the feared image and the body’s reaction in a paced, controlled way, never forcing exposure.

### Habits and behaviour change

Smoking, nail biting, emotional eating, procrastination, checking your phone. We look at what the habit is doing for you, then build a kinder replacement.

## When hypnotherapy is not the right fit

Hypnotherapy is not a substitute for medical or psychiatric treatment. If you are experiencing severe mental health difficulties, active psychosis, epilepsy that is not medically managed, or you are in crisis, please contact your GP or emergency services first. I am always happy to talk through whether this work is appropriate for you.
