## Work with what is alive for you

You do not have to fit neatly into a category. Many people arrive with a mix of stress, poor sleep and low confidence, or with a habit they have tried to change many times. We can work with the whole picture.

Most clients book a free intake first, then a series of three to six sessions. Some come for a single, focused session around a specific event. We decide together what makes sense — there is no fixed package you have to buy.

Every session lasts 60 minutes and includes time to talk, a guided relaxation and a short practice you can use at home. Sessions are available in person in Rotterdam or online via video.
