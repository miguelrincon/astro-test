## Who we are

Stillwater Hypnotherapy is a sole-trader practice run by Robin van Dijk, based in Rotterdam, the Netherlands. For questions about this policy or your data, email hello@stillwaterhypnotherapy.com.

## What we collect

When you contact us or book an intake, we may collect your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. During sessions, we keep brief professional notes about the topics we work on. We do not record sessions unless you explicitly ask us to.

Our website does not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. If we add analytics later, this policy will be updated before it goes live.

## Why we process your data

We use your data to respond to enquiries, schedule appointments, provide the service you have asked for, keep professional notes and meet our legal and insurance obligations. The lawful bases are consent, performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in running a safe practice.

## How long we keep it

Enquiry emails are kept for up to 12 months. Client notes are kept for the period required by our professional insurer and applicable law, and are then securely deleted.

## Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is only shared with processors who help us operate the practice — for example our email provider, booking tool and accountant — under appropriate agreements. We may disclose information if required by law.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us to make a request. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

## Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted email, password-protected devices and access controls. No system is completely secure, but we treat your information with care.

## Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available on this page.
